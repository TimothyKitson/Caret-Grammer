Caret - where the word lists come from
======================================

Caret's checking runs on four data files in this folder. They are built from
the public sources below by tools/build-data.py and tools/hunspell-expand.py
in the Caret source repository, then filtered and added to by hand.


words.txt - the dictionary
--------------------------
* dwyl/english-words
  https://github.com/dwyl/english-words
  Released into the public domain (the Unlicense).

* The en_US Hunspell dictionary from SCOWL, via wooorm/dictionaries
  http://wordlist.aspell.net/  https://github.com/wooorm/dictionaries
  Used for word forms (plurals, tenses) the base list was missing.
  Copyright Kevin Atkinson and others; the full notice, which must travel
  with any copy, is in LICENSE-hunspell-en_US.txt next to this file.

* Words added for Caret (tools/sources/extra-words.txt).


propernouns.txt - names, places and brands that take a capital
---------------------------------------------------------------
* The capitalized entries of the same en_US Hunspell dictionary (SCOWL),
  under the notice in LICENSE-hunspell-en_US.txt, plus additions for Caret.


frequency.txt - how common each word is
---------------------------------------
* hermitdave/FrequencyWords, 2018 English list
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  Word counts from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/).
  Code MIT, (c) 2016 Hermit Dave. Content licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

  frequency.txt is an adaptation of that list (trimmed and filtered) and is
  itself licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/


misspellings.json - common typos and their fixes
------------------------------------------------
* Wikipedia: Lists of common misspellings / For machines
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Lists_of_common_misspellings/For_machines
  By Wikipedia contributors, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

  misspellings.json is an adaptation of that list (entries that are also
  ordinary words removed, format changed) and is itself licensed under
  CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
