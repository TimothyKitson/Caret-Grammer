What it does

A quiet second pair of eyes

No sign-up, no subscription, and nothing sent anywhere unless you switch on the AI pass. The whole dictionary ships inside the extension.

We will definately Spelling “definately” is a common misspelling. definitely Add to dictionaryIgnore be there. Thanks for the invite!

Hover, then decide

A card shows what is wrong and what to put instead. Nothing changes until you click it — Caret never rewrites what you wrote.

Everywhere you type

Gmail, Outlook, forms, comment boxes, the little search field nobody checks. If you can type in it, Caret watches it.

You type
i dont think so. maybe later
It becomes
I don't think so. Maybe later

Autocorrect, if you want it

Off by default. Switch it on and the mistakes with exactly one right answer fix themselves: i → I, dont → don't, capitals after a full stop. Ctrl+Z undoes any of them.

Private by default

The built-in checks run inside your browser and send nothing. Password fields are skipped, and card numbers and keys are blanked out even when the optional AI pass is on.

This is good. The IEP meeting is on Friday.

It knows when to keep quiet

A checker that underlines correct writing is worse than no checker. “This is good” is not a surname, IEP is not a typo, and a word you tell it to leave alone stays left alone — by the AI too.

An AI pass when you ask optional

For the sentence-level things a rule cannot see. Caret's own is free with a daily limit and nothing to sign up for; or bring your own key from Google, OpenAI or Anthropic. It is told to keep your words plain, never to make them sound fancier.

Getting started

Two minutes, start to finish

  1. 1

    Install it from the Edge store

    The button below opens the listing. Press Get, then Add extension.

  2. 2

    Start typing

    That is genuinely it. Mistakes get underlined; hover one to fix it, or press Ctrl Shift Space with the cursor in the word.

  3. 3

    Make it yours, if you like

    The settings page has autocorrect, what to check, your own dictionary and the optional AI pass. The setup guide walks through all of it.
