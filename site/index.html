What it does

A quiet second pair of eyes

No sign-up, no subscription, no sending your writing anywhere. The whole dictionary ships inside the extension.

Everywhere you type

Gmail, Outlook, forms, comment boxes, the little search field nobody checks. If you can type in it, Caret watches it.

Hover, then decide

A card shows what is wrong and what to put instead. Nothing changes until you click it — Caret never rewrites what you wrote.

Autocorrect, if you want it

Off by default. Switch it on and the mistakes with exactly one right answer fix themselves: i → I, dont → don't, capitals after a full stop. Ctrl+Z undoes any of them.

It stays on your computer

The checking runs inside your browser. Password fields, card numbers and anything that looks like a key are skipped entirely.

An AI pass when you askoptional

For the sentence-level things a rule cannot see. Bring a key from Google, OpenAI or Anthropic — Google's free tier needs no card. It is told to keep your words plain, never to make them sound fancier.

It knows when to keep quiet

A checker that underlines correct writing is worse than no checker. “This is good” is not a surname, IEP is not a typo, and your own words can be added to the dictionary in one click.

Getting started

Two minutes, start to finish

  1. Install it from the Edge store

    The button below opens the listing. Press Get, then Add extension.

  2. Start typing

    That is genuinely it. Mistakes get underlined; hover one to fix it, or press Alt G with the cursor in the word.

  3. Make it yours, if you like

    The settings page has autocorrect, what to check, your own dictionary and the optional AI pass. The setup guide walks through all of it.
