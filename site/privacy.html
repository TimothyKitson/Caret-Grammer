Privacy

What Caret does with your writing

Short version: nothing leaves your computer unless you switch on the AI pass and press the button.

The checking

The dictionary, the frequency list and every rule ship inside the extension. Checking happens in your own browser. There is no Caret server, no account, and no analytics — I do not know how many people use this or what they type.

What is stored

Your settings, the words you add to your dictionary, the sites you turn it off on, the suggestions you have ignored, and a few counters for the Numbers section. All of it lives in your browser profile's extension storage. Removing the extension removes all of it.

What is never looked at

Password fields are skipped outright. So is any field a site marks as not wanting a checker. Text that looks like a card number, an API key or similar is left alone even when the AI pass is on.

The optional AI pass

Off unless you turn it on and add your own API key. When you do, the contents of the field you are typing in are sent to the service that key belongs to — Google, OpenAI or Anthropic — and that service's own terms apply to it. The key is kept in your browser profile and sent to nobody else.

Sent with it is a list of words you have told Caret to leave alone: the words in your dictionary, and the ones you have pressed Ignore on. That is what stops the AI correcting the way you write on purpose. It is a list of your own choices, not anything inferred from your writing, and it goes to the same place the text does and nowhere else.

By default it only runs when you ask for it.

This website

Hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs. The only form on the site is the report form, and what you type into it is emailed to me. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No tracking, no cookies set by me.
