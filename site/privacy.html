What Caret does with your writing

Short version: the built-in checks never send anything anywhere. Only the optional AI pass does, and only once you switch it on.

The checking

The dictionary, the frequency list and every rule ship inside the extension, and the checking happens in your own browser. No account, no analytics, no tracking — I do not know how many people use this or what they type.

What is stored

Your settings, the words you add to your dictionary, the sites you turn it off on, the suggestions you have ignored, how many AI checks you have used today, and a few counters for the Numbers section. All of it lives in your browser profile's extension storage. Removing the extension removes all of it.

What is never sent

Password fields are skipped outright. So is any field a site marks as not wanting a checker. When the AI pass is on, card numbers, social security numbers, passwords written out (“my password is …”) and anything that looks like an API key or token are blanked out before the text leaves your computer.

The optional AI pass

Off until you switch it on. When the full check is on, the text of the field you are working in is sent to the AI you picked in the settings — by default only when you ask for it, or on the schedule you chose instead.

Caret (the free, built-in option): the text goes to a small server I run on Cloudflare, which passes it to Cloudflare Workers AI and sends the suggested corrections back. My server does not store or log your text. The only things it keeps are two numbers per day — how many full checks and how many quick checks the key has made — with no record of who made them. Cloudflare handles the request under its own terms.

The quick check is separate, and also off until you switch it on. When it is on, each sentence you finish is sent on its own to that same Caret server on Cloudflare, which passes it to a small model on Cloudflare Workers AI and sends any corrections back. It goes there whichever AI you picked for the full check. It is not stored or logged either, and card numbers, passwords and anything that looks like a key are blanked out before it leaves your computer, exactly as for the full check.

Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic: the text goes straight from your browser to that company, using your own key, and that company's terms apply. The key is kept in your browser profile and sent to nobody else.

Sent with it is a list of words you have told Caret to leave alone: the words in your dictionary, and the ones you have pressed Ignore on. That is what stops the AI correcting the way you write on purpose. It is a list of your own choices, not anything inferred from your writing, and it goes to the same place the text does and nowhere else. With the quick check, the list is cut down to the words that appear in that one sentence.

Removing Caret

When you remove the extension, Edge opens a page on this site asking why. Answering is optional. The address of that page carries the version of Caret you had, and nothing else.

This website

Hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs. The only form on the site is the report form, and what you type into it is emailed to me. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No tracking, no cookies set by me.
