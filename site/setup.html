Setup

Setting up Caret

Caret works the moment it is installed. Everything on this page is optional — it is here for when you want to change something.

  1. Install it

    Open the Edge Add-ons listing, press Get, then Add extension. Edge will ask once — that is the permission to read the text boxes it checks.

    Get Caret for Edge

  2. Pin it to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon beside the address bar, find Caret, and click the pin. The button gives you the on/off switches, a Check with AI button, and a way into the settings.

  3. Open the settings

    Click the Caret button, then Settings. The page has a search box at the top left — type what you are looking for rather than hunting for it.

    At the very top is a live preview: a sentence run through the real checker. Change anything below and the preview updates, so you can see what a setting does before you commit to it.

  4. Decide what gets underlined

    Five switches under What to check: spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, and an extra picky one for passive voice and filler words, which is noisier and off by default.

    If names keep getting underlined, leave Flag names and unusual words off — that is what it is for.

  5. Turn on autocorrect, if you want it

    Off until you switch it on. It only fixes mistakes with exactly one right answer:

    Capitalize I — i and ive become I and I've, never ill or id, because those are words.
    Sentence capitals — after a full stop, a question mark or a new line. Not after Dr. or a trailing-off ….
    Apostrophes — dont → don't, wouldnt → wouldn't.
    Known typos — teh → the.

    Anything with a judgement call in it — their/there, your/you're — is still only underlined. Every correction is an ordinary edit, so Ctrl Z undoes it, and a word you undo is left alone from then on.

  6. Stop the double underlines

    Edge underlines in red too, and two sets of underlines disagreeing with each other is worse than either alone. Turn off the browser's own spell check is on by default: Caret silences Edge in the fields it is checking, and puts it back when you move on. A site that ships its own checker — Google Docs, say — cannot be switched off from here.

  7. Add your own words

    Surnames, street names, anything Caret keeps underlining that is not wrong. Either add them under My dictionary in the settings, or right-click the word on the page and choose Add to dictionary.

  8. Switch Caret off where you do not want it

    Right-click any page and choose Turn off on this site. The list lives in the settings under Turned off on, and you can remove one with the × beside it.

Optional

The AI pass

The built-in checks handle spelling, confused words, apostrophes, agreement and capitals on their own, offline and free. The AI pass is for the sentence-level things a rule cannot see — and it is genuinely optional.

Pick which one you want in the settings. Caret's own is free; the others use a key of your own, and Caret works out which service a key belongs to from the key itself:

Caretfree

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay. Someone gives you a key starting caret_ and you paste it once. It runs on a small shared allowance, so Caret holds you to 50 checks a day; when they are gone it goes quiet until midnight UTC and the built-in checks carry on as normal.

Google Geminifree tier

A key from aistudio.google.com/apikey needs no card and no credits. There is a cap per minute and per day, which proofreading one field at a time does not come near. Keys start AIza.

OpenAI

Pay as you go — you have to buy credits before the key does anything. There is no free allowance. Keys start sk-.

Anthropic

Also pay as you go. Keys start sk-ant-.

  1. Pick one, then paste the key

    Settings → AI proofreading → switch it on, choose which AI, then paste the key. It is kept in your browser profile and sent only to the service it belongs to. If the key does not match the service you picked, Caret says so rather than letting the API fail at you later.

  2. Choose when it runs

    This is the setting that decides your bill, so it is a real choice:

    Only when I ask — right-click the box and choose Proofread with AI, press Ctrl Shift ., or use the Check with AI button in the toolbar popup. Nothing is sent until you do. This is the default.
    Every minute while I am typing — one check a minute at most, and only in a field you are actually working in.
    After every pause — the most thorough and by far the most expensive.

  3. Know what the limit is

    On a free option Caret holds you to 50 checks a day on this computer — you can set it lower, not higher, because the allowance behind it belongs to somebody else. On a paid key of your own it does not limit you at all, though you can still set a number so a stuck key cannot quietly run up a bill.

Whatever the model suggests, it may only swap a word or a short phrase — never rewrite a sentence — and it is told to keep your vocabulary plain rather than make it sound more formal. Nothing is applied until you click it.

Reference

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl Shift Space

Suggestions for the word the cursor is in.

Ctrl Shift .

Proofread this field with AI.

Right-click

Proofread with AI, right there in the menu — no shortcut to remember.

Esc

Close the suggestion card.

You can change any of these: settings → Keyboard and buttons → Change the shortcuts.
