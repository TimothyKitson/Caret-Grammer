Setting up Caret

Caret works the moment it is installed. Everything on this page is optional — it is here for when you want to change something.

Step by step

  1. Install it

    Open the Edge Add-ons listing, press Get, then Add extension. Edge will ask once — that is the permission to read the text boxes it checks.

    Get Caret for Edge

  2. Pin it to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon beside the address bar, find Caret, and click the pin. The button gives you the on/off switches, a Check with AI button, and a way into the settings.

  3. Open the settings

    Click the Caret button, then Settings. The page has a search box at the top left — type what you are looking for rather than hunting for it.

    At the very top is a live preview: a sentence run through the real checker. Change anything below and the preview updates, so you can see what a setting does before you commit to it.

  4. Decide what gets underlined

    Five switches under What to check: spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, and an extra picky one for passive voice and filler words, which is noisier and off by default.

    If names keep getting underlined, leave Flag names and unusual words off — that is what it is for.

  5. Turn on autocorrect, if you want it

    Off until you switch it on. It only fixes mistakes with exactly one right answer:

    Capitalize I
    i and ive become I and I've, never ill or id, because those are words.
    Sentence capitals
    After a full stop, a question mark or a new line. Not after Dr. or a trailing-off ….
    Apostrophes
    dont → don't, wouldnt → wouldn't.
    Known typos
    teh → the.

    Anything with a judgement call in it — their/there, your/you're — is still only underlined. Every correction is an ordinary edit, so Ctrl Z undoes it, and a word you undo is left alone from then on.

  6. Stop the double underlines

    Edge underlines in red too, and two sets of underlines disagreeing with each other is worse than either alone. Turn off the browser's own spell check is on by default: Caret silences Edge in the fields it is checking, and puts it back when you move on. A site that ships its own checker — Google Docs, say — cannot be switched off from here.

  7. Add your own words

    Surnames, street names, anything Caret keeps underlining that is not wrong. Either add them under My dictionary in the settings — type a name with its capitals and Caret keeps them — or hover the underlined word on the page and press Add to dictionary on the card.

  8. Switch Caret off where you do not want it

    Right-click any page and choose Turn checking on or off for this site, or paste the address into the settings under Turned off on. A site on the list covers its subdomains too, and you can remove one with the × beside it.

Optional

The AI pass

The built-in checks handle spelling, confused words, apostrophes, agreement and capitals on their own, offline and free. The AI pass is for the sentence-level things a rule cannot see — and it is genuinely optional.

Pick which one you want in the settings. Caret's own is free and built in; the others use a key of your own, and Caret works out which service a key belongs to from the key itself:

  • Caret

    free

    Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay and nothing to paste: the copy from the Edge store has it built in. Your text goes to Caret's small server on Cloudflare, which asks Cloudflare's AI and sends the corrections back without keeping a copy. It runs on a small shared allowance, so Caret holds you to 20 full checks a day, plus the quick check if you turn it on; when they are gone it goes quiet until tomorrow and the built-in checks carry on as normal.

    Built in — no key needed

  • Google Gemini

    free tier

    A key from aistudio.google.com/apikey needs no card and no credits. There is a cap per minute and per day, which proofreading one field at a time does not come near.

    Keys start AIza

  • OpenAI

    pay as you go

    Pay as you go — you have to buy credits before the key does anything. There is no free allowance.

    Keys start sk-

  • Anthropic

    pay as you go

    Also pay as you go.

    Keys start sk-ant-

  1. Pick one, and paste a key if it needs one

    Settings → AI proofreading → switch it on and choose which AI. Caret's own is ready straight away. For the others, paste your key: it is kept in your browser profile and sent only to the service it belongs to, and if it does not match the service you picked, Caret says so rather than letting the API fail at you later.

  2. Choose when it runs

    On a paid key this is the setting that decides your bill, and on a free one how long the day's checks last, so it is a real choice:

    Only when I ask default
    Right-click the box and choose Proofread with AI, press Ctrl Shift ., or use the Check with AI button in the toolbar popup. Nothing is sent until you do. This is the default.
    Every minute while I am typing
    One check a minute at most, and only in a field you are actually working in.
    After every pause
    The most thorough and by far the most expensive.

    On Caret's own, with 20 full checks a day, the two automatic choices use them up quickly. Leave it on Only when I ask, and let the quick check do the checking as you type.

  3. Know what the limit is

    On a free option Caret holds you to a number of full checks a day on this computer: 20 on Caret's own, because its free allowance is shared with everybody who uses it, and 50 on Google's free tier. You can set it lower, not higher. On a paid key of your own it does not limit you at all, though you can still set a number so a stuck key cannot quietly run up a bill.

    The quick check has a number of its own, below, and does not use these up.

  4. Check whole sentences too new

    Settings → AI proofreading → Check sentences too. The full check then also points out run-on sentences, fragments, missing words and wording that is hard to follow. The whole stretch gets a soft amber highlight, and the word mistakes inside it keep their own underlines. A small fix — a full stop, a comma, one missing word — gets a button; anything bigger is explained, and left for you to reword. It is off until you switch it on, and it does not use any extra checks.

  5. Let it learn how you write new

    With Learn how I write on — it is, unless you switch it off — Caret waits five minutes after a full check, then shows the AI the suggestions you took and the ones you ignored, along with what you wrote. The AI sends back nothing but a few short notes on your style: British or American spelling, say, or that you start sentences with “And” on purpose. Those notes go with every full check after that, so it stops suggesting what you keep turning down.

    The notes are kept on your computer. Read them under What Caret has learned, and press Forget all of it to start again.

  6. Turn on the quick check, if you want it

    A second, much smaller AI that reads one sentence at a time. Once you finish a sentence — a full stop, a question mark or an exclamation mark — and move on, or stop typing for a moment, Caret sends just that sentence and underlines anything that comes back. A paragraph of five sentences is five quick checks.

    Checked once
    A sentence is not sent again unless you change it and then move on from it.
    Free
    It always runs on Caret's own server, whichever AI you picked for the full check. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay.
    30 a day, up to 80
    New installs get 30 quick checks a day. Raise it to as many as 80 under Quick checks a day, or set it lower.
    With or without the full check
    It has its own switch. Have the quick check on, the full check on, or both.

    Settings → AI proofreading → Check each sentence as I finish it. It is off until you switch it on. It shares one free allowance with everybody else, so on a very busy day it can go quiet until midnight UTC; the built-in checks carry on as normal.

Whatever the model suggests, it may only swap a word or a short phrase — never rewrite a sentence — and it is told to keep your vocabulary plain rather than make it sound more formal. Nothing is applied until you click it.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl Shift Space
Suggestions for the word the cursor is in.
Ctrl Shift .
Proofread this field with AI.
Right-click
Proofread with AI, right there in the menu — no shortcut to remember.
Esc
Close the suggestion card.

You can change any of these: settings → Keyboard and buttons → Change the shortcuts.
