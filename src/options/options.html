Autocorrect off by default

Caret usually just underlines mistakes. Turn this on and it fixes the easy ones for you as you type. Stuff like their and there still gets underlined so you can pick.

AI optional

The AI is really good at finding spelling and grammar mistakes. It won't change anything for you. You have to do that. None of your personal information, like passwords, credit cards, or API keys, goes outside of the text box.

It learns what you don't want fixed. Words in your dictionary and stuff you clicked Ignore on get sent too, so the AI leaves them alone.

You don't need this. Caret already catches a lot without it. This is just for the stuff it can't catch on its own.
