Autocorrect off by default

Caret normally underlines a mistake and waits for you. With this on, the few mistakes that have exactly one right answer get fixed the moment you finish the word — no click, no card. Anything with a judgement call in it (their/there, your/you’re) is still only underlined.

AI proofreading optional

The built-in checks are instant, offline and free. This one sends the contents of the field to an AI provider and adds what it finds to the underlines. It never rewrites anything: it may swap a word or a short phrase, it is told to keep your vocabulary plain rather than make it sound fancier, and nothing changes until you click the suggestion. Password fields, card numbers and anything that looks like a key are never sent.

It learns what to leave alone. Every word you add to your dictionary, and every suggestion you dismiss with Ignore, is sent with the request as a list of things not to touch. So the AI stops re-proposing a change you have already turned down, and stops correcting the way you write on purpose. Nothing is guessed from your writing — only the choices you made yourself.

You do not need this. The built-in checks handle spelling, confused words, apostrophes, agreement and capitals on their own, for free, with no account. This is for the sentence-level things a rule cannot see.

API key Paste any one of these. Caret works out which from the key itself and sends it nowhere else.
  • A Caret key nothing to sign up for If someone gave you a key starting caret_, paste that and you are done — no account anywhere, nothing to pay. It runs on a small shared allowance, so it goes quiet for the rest of the day if that runs out.
  • Google Gemini free tier A key from aistudio.google.com/apikey needs no card and no credits. There is a cap per minute and per day, which proofreading one field at a time does not come near. Starts AIza.
  • OpenAI Pay as you go — you have to buy credits first, there is no free allowance. Starts sk-.
  • Anthropic Also pay as you go. Starts sk-ant-.
